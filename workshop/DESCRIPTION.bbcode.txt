[h1]Sleep Until[/h1]
Choose when to wake up in Graveyard Keeper 2: sleep normally, until the next morning, or until a chosen weekday.

[b]This is a BepInEx code mod, not a translation.[/b] The game's current uploader only offers the Translation category. Subscribing alone does not load this mod.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Normal: preserve the game's usual sleep behavior.
[*]Till morning: wake at the next 6 a.m.
[*]Choose any of the six weekdays and see the approximate wait.
[*]Scheduled sleep works at full energy.
[*]Wake early with F8 or the on-screen Wake now button.
[*]Keep the game's normal accelerated world simulation and wake/save path.
[/list]
World processes continue while you sleep. Long sleeps can pass over events. Before 6 a.m., selecting today's weekday targets this morning; at or after 6 a.m., it targets next week.

[h2]Installation[/h2]
Requires Windows x64, Graveyard Keeper 2, and [url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases/tag/v5.4.23.5]BepInEx 5.4.23.5[/url] installed in the game folder.

[list]
[*]Manual: copy this item's BepInEx\plugins\SleepUntil folder into the game's BepInEx\plugins folder. See INSTALL.txt.
[*]Optional: install [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3807346541]GK2 Workshop Loader[/url] using its guide, subscribe, and approve the mod. This package follows its author guide; we have not tested that integration in-game.
[/list]
Do not keep both manual and Workshop-managed copies. Back up your saves before adding mods.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
v0.1.0 has been reported working in-game. Other sleep/time mods (including Better Sleep), co-op, all controller setups, future game versions, and other operating systems have not been fully tested. No GK2 Mod Framework requirement. No game DLLs are bundled.
